Privacy policy

Mirror Up, by Unfortunate Name Studios. Last updated 1 October 2026.

The short version

Mirror Up collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours that the app talks to.

What the app touches

  • Your camera. The app talks to the camera over the USB cable, and only after you pick it in the browser's permission dialog. Nothing from the camera leaves your phone.
  • Photos. A photo you take is transferred from the camera to the phone and shown to you. It stays in the phone's memory until you save it, share it, or take the next one. Saving writes it to your phone's storage; sharing hands it to the app you choose. The app itself keeps no copy.
  • Settings. Your preferences (grid, focus peaking, focus step sizes and the like) are kept in the browser's local storage on your phone. They never leave the device.
  • Screen wake lock. The app asks the phone to keep the screen on while live view is running.

Hosting

The app is a web page served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of the request) when the app's files are fetched. That is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. After the first visit the app runs from a cache on your phone and works with no connection at all.

Permissions

The app asks for USB access to reach the camera. It asks for nothing else: no location, no contacts, no microphone, no phone camera.

Children

The app is not directed at children and, as it collects nothing, holds no information about anyone of any age.

Changes
